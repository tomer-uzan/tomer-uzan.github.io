Seven repos

One repo became seven. Each has one job.

the CRM
the product: listings, buyers, matches, WhatsApp, signatures
the mobile app
the agent in the field, iPhone and Android
the site
the public face, nexusrealty.app
ops
the back office: plans, billing, support, staff
the ad studio
video and still ads, rendered from code
the docs
every screen, button and rule, written down. The gates every pull request must pass
the HQ
a watchman: every five minutes it checks every product and tells me what broke

Three roads lead to the CRM: the app, the site, ops. Nothing shares a database.
