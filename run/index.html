Run

In the summer of 2025 I could not run one kilometer.
I worked from morning to night, ate junk food and did not move.

So I made a bet at work: 10 kilometers in 45 minutes, within a year.

Six runs a week. After a month I ran 10 kilometers.
By February: 46:50.

Then the heat came, and an injury. I lost the bet and I paid.
I never stopped running.

Today: 70 kilometers a week, 16 kilos lighter.
The goal: 100 miles.
